Kashtan (Chestnut Tree)

78 Engelsa Iela (now Stabu Iela), Riga, Latvia 1984

A long (maybe 300 meters) straight dirt and gravel road with rusting one story Soviet garazhes on the left and small gardens of the old ladies inhabiting the apartment building at the end of the road on the right. At the end of the road, to the right, a glorious old Chestnut Tree, taller than the building itself. 

The building is a rectangular three story squat dilapidated grey stucco apartment building, build in the 1920s for the workers of the surrounding small-scale factories, with no architecture to speak of: it is showing signs of water damage and has two entries. Width-wise it has two windows, first entry with staircase shaft windows above it, then four windows, the second entry with straircase shaft windows, and two more windows (so three rows of that, since it is a three story building).

As kids we completely adored the chestnut tree and played with it in all seasons. We climbed it, played pirate ship, or military observation post, roasted chestnuts in the fall under it, or just sat and looked at the star at night in the summer. My apartment was on the third floor in center right part of the building and I would spend a long time just contemplating this magnificent tree (maybe even writing poetry about it).

The game is a meditation on this tree: observing it at all seasons (bare and covered in snow in the winter, green and gorgeously blooming in white in the spring, deep green and teaming with birds in the summer, and turning yellow and brown in the fall with chestnuts falling from it), climbing it or observing it from my window. Meditative music.. Three js. Make it gorgeous and fun!
