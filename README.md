# Kashtan (Каштан)

A meditation on a chestnut tree — 78 Engelsa iela (now Stabu iela), Rīga, 1984.

*For my childhood friends — Ivars, Normunds, Zhanis, Laila and Ivonne.*

**Play:** https://rioffe.github.io/kashtan/

Come in from Stabu iela through the gateway and walk the gravel lane between the rusting garages and the old ladies' gardens to the
chestnut tree at the end. Watch it through four seasons and any time of day; climb it,
lie under it and look at the stars, sit at the third-floor window, kick the trunk, gather
chestnuts in autumn and roast them over a fire. Generative meditative music throughout.

Behind the garages and the gardens stand the squat red-brick workshops with their tin roofs; the
gardens are full of apple, pear and plum trees, red currant and gooseberry bushes; and all around,
as far as the eye can see, the five-storey buildings of Rīga's old centre. The neighbourhood cat —
black, with white paws and a white breast — turns up out of nowhere: on the garage roofs, in the
gardens (sometimes with a mouse or a bird), or dozing on a limb of the chestnut.

Clamber onto the garage roofs and run along them, scramble from there (or up a garden shed, or a
ladder someone left against the wall) onto the tin roofs of the workshops. In winter, build a
snowman — and come spring, watch him lean, lose his nose and melt into the grass.

Plays in English, Russian (Русский) or Latvian (Latviešu): pick on the title screen or with the
EN / RU / LV button (or `?lang=ru`, `?lang=lv`).

Single-file Three.js — open `index.html` or serve the folder.

## Controls
- **WASD / arrows** walk · **drag** to look · **Shift** run · **Space** jump / clamber onto a roof (or just walk into a garage)
- **1–4** seasons · **T** time of day · **V** window · **C** climb (W/S up/down) · **L** lie down · **Esc** walk
- **F** shake the tree (in your room: open the window) · **O** desk lamp · **R** roast chestnuts · **M** music
- **B** build a snowman (in winter) · **Y** weather: fair, cloudy, rain (snow in deep winter) · click a book in your room to open the Post machine
